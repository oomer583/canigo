# CanIGo demo: 3 directions (pick one)

Length: 2:20 (rules allow 2–3 min). 1920×1080, 30 fps. Every UI shot is real footage recorded with `video/rec.mjs` from the local build.
The story beats are the same in all three; what changes is the visual system, the camera and the cutting.

## A: Threshold (door-shaped mask)
- **Idea:** every shot is seen through an arched door aperture. The camera keeps pushing *through* the door into the next scene, so the whole film is one continuous move.
- **Type:** Instrument Serif (italic for emotion) + Inter Tight caps for labels. **Colour:** CanIGo deep green #0b3d2e at night, accent yellow #f2c14e, verdict colours only inside the door.
- **Motion:** slow push-ins, mask morphs (one door splits into two at 1:00), words rise from behind a mask line. Mood: quiet, dignified, big emotional close.
- **Risk:** least "kinetic" of the three; it depends on precise camera moves.

## B: Signal (verdict colours, hard cuts)
- **Idea:** the three verdict colours are the whole visual language. Black stage, huge expanded grotesk, and hard cuts on every beat. At 1:00 the frame slams into a yellow/red split screen.
- **Type:** Archivo (expanded 125 for shouts, condensed 62 for asides) + JetBrains Mono for data (source, date, cm, steps).
- **Motion:** type that slides, stretches (width axis) and masks on beat; colour wipes as transitions; UI cards drop in with real shadow. Mood: most energetic. This is the answer to "not kinetic".
- **Risk:** the loudest one; it needs a track with a clear pulse (about 100–120 BPM).

## C: Field notes (one take on a paper canvas)
- **Idea:** all the real clips sit on one big paper table; one continuous camera glides, rotates and zooms between them. Mono annotations with hairline leaders mark sources, dates and step counts.
- **Type:** Newsreader serif + JetBrains Mono. **Colour:** app paper #efece4, ink #15201b, verdict dots.
- **Motion:** a continuous camera path (Katana-style) with depth (perspective grid, parallax layers); kinetic type writes itself on beat. Mood: documentary, personal, precise.
- **Risk:** calmer energy in the middle; the close lands softer than in A.
